1. Introduction

Welcome to Ansera (https://cs5224-ansera.web.app, the "Site"). Ansera helps small businesses answer their customers on WhatsApp with an AI assistant, take orders and bookings, and send payment links. We understand that privacy matters, both to the businesses that use Ansera and to the people who message them.

This policy covers two groups of people:

  • Business Users: business owners and staff who sign up for and use the Ansera admin site.
  • Customers: people who message a business that uses Ansera on WhatsApp. For their information, we act on behalf of that business: the business decides what its assistant asks for and is responsible to its customers for how it uses their information.

2. Personal information

"Personal information" means information that identifies, or can be used to identify, contact or locate a person, such as a name, email address, phone number, delivery address or IP address. It does not include information that is anonymous or aggregated so that it can't be linked to a person.

3. What information we collect

From Business Users

  • Account details: name, email address and username. If you sign up with a password, we store only a secure hash of it. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, whether the email is verified, and an account identifier (see sections 4 and 5).
  • Business details: the business's name, location and description, and the products, services, prices, FAQs, opening hours and assistant settings you enter.
  • WhatsApp connection details: your WhatsApp Business phone number ID, display phone number and verified business name, plus the access token and app secret that let Ansera receive and send messages for your number.

From Customers (on behalf of the business)

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • The messages you exchange with the business's assistant, and replies the business adds.
  • Details you give to place an order or booking, as the business asks for them (for example your name, email address, delivery address, number of people or notes), and what you ordered or booked.
  • Payment status and a payment reference. We do not receive or store card or bank details: payments are made on our payment provider's page (see section 7).

Technical information

Our servers keep logs of requests, including IP address, browser type and time, to keep the service secure and to fix problems.

4. Google API Limited Use disclosure

When you choose "Continue with Google", we use Google Sign-In to receive your basic profile information: your name, email address, whether it is verified, and your Google account identifier. We use this information only to create your Ansera account and log you in, which are features visible to you in the Site. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar or any other Google Workspace data.

Ansera's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

5. Facebook and WhatsApp

  • Facebook Login: if you choose "Continue with Facebook", we receive your name, email address and Facebook user identifier, and use them only to create your account and log you in.
  • Connecting WhatsApp: when a business connects its WhatsApp Business account to Ansera (by entering its details, or through Meta's sign-up flow), we receive the identifiers of its WhatsApp Business account and phone number and an access token. We use them only to receive that business's customer messages and send its assistant's replies.
  • Messages: WhatsApp messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta's own terms and privacy policy also apply to them.

6. How we use information

  • To create and secure accounts and log Business Users in.
  • To run each business's assistant: read customer messages, write replies using the business's own products, services and FAQs, and record orders and bookings.
  • To show each business its own conversations, orders, bookings and payments.
  • To create payment links and send payment and appointment reminders for the business.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To contact Business Users about their account or the service.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or build profiles of people for marketing.

7. Service providers that process information

We rely on these providers to run Ansera. Each processes information only as needed for its service:

  • Google Cloud (Singapore): hosting, database, message queues, Firebase Hosting, and Identity Platform for Google and Facebook sign-in.
  • Google Vertex AI (Gemini): generates the assistant's replies. The customer's message, the conversation so far and the business's relevant information are sent to the model for each reply. Under Google Cloud's terms, Google does not use this data to train its models.
  • Meta Platforms: the WhatsApp Business Platform and Facebook Login.
  • Xendit: payments. Customers pay on Xendit's hosted page, which collects their payment details under Xendit's privacy policy; we receive only the payment status and reference.

8. When we share information

  • With the business you message: a Customer's messages, details, orders and bookings are visible to the business they were sent to, and to no other business.
  • With the service providers listed in section 7.
  • To comply with the law: when required by a court order, subpoena or lawful request from a public authority, or when reasonably necessary to protect someone's safety.

9. Storage and retention

Information is stored on Google Cloud servers in Singapore. We keep it while the business's account is active, so the business can see its conversations, orders and bookings. A business can disconnect WhatsApp at any time, which deletes its stored WhatsApp credentials immediately. When we receive a deletion request (section 13), we delete the information within 30 days, except where we must keep it to comply with the law. Residual copies in backups are removed as those backups expire.

10. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The admin site stores your login session and display preferences (such as light or dark mode) in your browser's local storage, so you stay logged in. The Google and Facebook sign-in windows may set their own cookies under those companies' policies.

11. Security

  • All connections to the Site and our services use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • WhatsApp access tokens and app secrets are encrypted at rest and are never shown again after they are saved.
  • Each business can access only its own data, and login sessions expire automatically.

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your choices

  • Business Users can view and change their business details in the Site, disconnect WhatsApp at any time, and contact us to correct, export or delete their account information.
  • Customers can stop messaging a business at any time, and can ask the business (or us) to correct or delete their information or to stop sending them reminders.

13. Deleting your data

To have your personal information deleted:

  1. Email us at the address in Contact us with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Tell us the email address of your Ansera account, or, if you are a Customer, your WhatsApp phone number and the business you messaged.
  3. We confirm the request with you, then delete your information within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Ansera under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites; then email us as above so we delete the information we already hold. If you signed in with Google, you can remove access at your Google Account's third-party connections.

14. Children

Ansera is a service for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use or disclose personal information, we will also notify Business Users by email before it takes effect.

16. Links to other sites

The Site links to other websites, such as Meta's and Google's. When you follow a link you leave the Site, and that website's own privacy policy applies.
